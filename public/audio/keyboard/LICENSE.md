# Keyboard recordings

32 single-key recordings from **Keyboard Soundpack #1 — Typing and Single
Keystrokes**, by **unicae_games**.

Original source: https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes

License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These files were redistributed in the latent-spaces/brag asset library as
`keypress-001.wav` through `keypress-032.wav`, with the original CC0 provenance
documented at https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag/blob/main/skills/brag/references/audio.md.
`recordings.json` preserves the original WAV bytes, base64 encoded in sample-number
order for one cached request. Separate WAV copies are omitted to avoid duplication.
The concatenated decoded recordings total 808,320 bytes and have SHA-256
`b5cdbd45c1099ec241276fac1ab467baed3acfbd0e3788b03b0d8b4929a16ccd`.
This pack avoids
download managers intercepting individual WAV requests. Soft applies a playback low-pass filter;
the key groups use small playback-rate differences. These are treatments of the
same recording set, not recordings of distinct branded switches.
